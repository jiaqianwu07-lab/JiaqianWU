Communication storytelling and AI

My work began with journalism and all-media communication, where I first developed skills in writing, storytelling, interviewing, visual expression, and cross-platform communication. Over time, I came to see communication not simply as information delivery, but as a process that shapes how people understand the world, build connections, and make sense of what matters.

I have long been interested in how content is packaged, how stories are framed, and how overlooked issues, cultures, and voices can gain clearer expression in media environments. Whether through an independent bookstore content project, field research on intangible cultural heritage, internships in media organizations, or research-driven work on AI, recommendation systems, misinformation, and digital products, I aim to build more meaningful connections between communication, the humanities, and technology.

As my learning path expanded, I began systematic training in generative AI, Python, data analysis, data mining, algorithmic culture, AI ethics, and digital journalism. These experiences encouraged me to think not only about what AI can do, but also about how it reshapes platforms, visibility, trust, governance, and public understanding. What interests me most now is working at the intersection of AI, digital media, and storytelling, where I can continue exploring projects and practices that are both technically grounded and socially meaningful.
